## Short Biography
I am Soto Anno (安納 爽響), a researcher at the Network Service Systems Laboratories, NTT Inc. I obtained a Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science at the Institute of Science Tokyo (previously known as Tokyo Institute of Technology).

My research interests include a data-driven understanding/modeling of real-world environments, such as urban environments, human mobilities, and our activities in cities. My research interests also include machine learning, deep learning, big data analysis, geographic information systems, urban computing, context-aware computing, and computer vision.

Member of [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE)](https://www.ieee.org/), [Institute of Electronics, Information and Communication Engineers (IEICE)](https://www.ieice.org/eng_r/index.html), [Information Processing Society of Japan (IPSJ)](https://www.ipsj.or.jp/english/index.html).
